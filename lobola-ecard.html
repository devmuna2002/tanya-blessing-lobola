The day's order

Order of Ceremony

"Roora" programme

  • 11:30 AM

    Arrival of Families

    Uncles, aunts and elders of both houses arrive and take their places on the "dare" (council).

  • 12:30 PM

    Kudzana — The Formal Entry

    Guests are welcomed and the lobola negotiations (roora) begin, led by the vakuru (elders) of each family.

  • 01:30 PM

    Masungiro — The Binding

    The bride price is presented and honoured in dance, song and ululation as the two houses are joined.

  • 02:30 PM

    Amai & Mai Hombe Tribute

    Blessings and words of wisdom from mothers and grandmothers; the hunter (hunhu) spirit is honoured.

  • 03:30 PM

    Mabiko — Feast & Celebration

    A traditional feast is shared, followed by gifts, music and celebration well into the afternoon.

"Mapfumo anorwa muhondo, asi mwana wehuku anorwira mukati megembe" —
The strong fight their wars publicly, but a chicken's chick fights within the calabash —
may your union be sheltered in the warmth of family.

A guide for our guests

Customs & Etiquette

Hunhu netsika

  • 1
    Keepsakes — a small gift or token for the couple is welcome; envelopes are presented to the family elders at the gift table.
  • 2
    Respect the dare — the negotiations are a joyful spectacle; applaud and celebrate but allow the elders to lead.
  • 3
    Camera etiquette — our photographers capture the day; please be mindful during the exchange of roora.
  • 4
    Feast etiquette — food is served in waves (kugovera chikafu); please stay seated and enjoy the procession of dishes.
  • 5
    Celebrate in song — learn a few lines of "Tipeiwo, madzibaba" and be ready to ululate (kuhwina) when the bride-price is counted!
